## 2025-02-18 - Accessibility for Hidden Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (such as Quick View, Wishlist, Compare, and the Cart icon) must have `aria-label`s for screen readers and visible focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`) for keyboard accessibility. Furthermore, interactive elements that are visually hidden until hovered (e.g., using `group-hover:opacity-100`) remain completely invisible to keyboard users when focused, unless explicitly styled to become visible on focus (e.g., adding `focus-visible:opacity-100`).
**Action:** Always add `aria-label`, `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none` to icon-only buttons. Whenever an element's visibility relies on hover (like `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`), ensure there is a corresponding `focus-visible` or `focus-within` utility (like `focus-visible:opacity-100`) to guarantee it becomes visible to keyboard-only users.
