## 2024-05-24 - Floating Widgets Accessibility\n**Learning:** Floating action widgets and chat interfaces (e.g. LiveChatWidget) are highly prone to missing accessible labels on state-toggling icon buttons. Because these components sit above the page content and often rely on simple icons (chat bubble, minimize, close), they create an invisible trap for screen reader users and those relying on tooltips for context.\n**Action:** Always audit floating action widgets and chat interfaces specifically for `aria-label` and `title` on any icon-only buttons, especially those that toggle the component's state (open/close/minimize).
