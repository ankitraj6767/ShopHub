## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility: Added ARIA labels to ProductCard buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons for actions like Wishlist, Compare, Quick View, and Add to Cart in the ProductCard component were missing `aria-label` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers.\n**Action:** Add descriptive, dynamic `aria-label` attributes to all icon-only interactive elements to ensure screen readers provide accurate context.
